# About GigaLab

GigaLab is a personal technical blog focused on hands-on labs, architectures and troubleshooting across Azure, hybrid cloud, networking and security.

The goal is simple: document configurations that have been tested in practice, explain what failed along the way, and provide repeatable validation steps.

## About the author

I'm Giovanni Garganese, a Cloud Solution Architect. I write about real-world cloud and hybrid infrastructure, with a particular focus on Azure, Azure Local, networking, observability and security.

> The views and opinions expressed on this site are my own and do not necessarily reflect those of my employer.
